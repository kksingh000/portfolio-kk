If the user invokes this skill without any other guidance, ask them what they want to build or design, ask some questions, and act as an expert designer who outputs HTML artifacts _or_ production code, depending on the need.

Non-negotiable rule for this brand: shell white `#F5F4F0`, near-black `#0A0A0A` and the gray ramp are the only colors in a default state. Teal `#2DD4CF` appears **only** on hover, press, focus, or a currently-highlighted element — one at a time. No emoji, ever.
